Cactus plants are mostly native to the arid regions of the Americas,
The fleshy stems store water,
The leaves degenerate into spines to reduce evaporation and defend against animals,
Some species bloom brightly.
They are extremely drought tolerant, have open stomata at night to conserve water, and often propagate cuttings.
In addition to ornamental, some varieties of fruit (such as cactus fruit) are edible,
The stems can also be used as fodder or medicinal.
