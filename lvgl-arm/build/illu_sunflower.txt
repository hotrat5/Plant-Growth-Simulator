Sunflower is an annual herbaceous plant of the Asteraceae family, native to North America, with erect stems,
The large flower disc consists of yellow tongue-like flowers and brown tubular flowers, up to 30 cm in diameter.
The flower disc at the seedling stage will rotate with the sun,
When ripe, the pollen is fixed in a south-easterly direction due to the fear of high temperatures.
Its seeds can be extracted for oil or eaten directly (such as sunflower seeds),
It is not only an important cash crop, but also a common ornamental flower.
