Rafflesia is a parasitic plant in the tropical rainforests of Southeast Asia, without rhizomes and leaves.
Survive only on giant flowers attached to grape plants, with flower diameters of up to 1 meter,
It is blood-red and smells of carrion, which attracts flies for pollination.
Although it is called a "man-eating flower", it does not actually prey on animals.
The flowering period is only 4-5 days, and it is on the verge of extinction due to habitat destruction.
It is a strange parasitic wonder of nature.
